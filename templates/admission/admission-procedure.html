Admission Procedure

Registration for classes Pre-Nursery to IX begins in the second week of September every year.

Age Criteria

Nursery 3 Yrs
K.G. 4 Yrs
Prep 5 Yrs
Class I 6 Yrs

Entrance Criteria

Nursery, KG & Prep

Parents and child meet the Vice Principal for an informal interaction, along with the birth certificate.

Class I to V

Admission is granted after a written test in Hindi, English, and Mathematics.

Class VI to IX

Written test is based on the previous class syllabus in Science, English, and Mathematics.

Class XI

Registration opens after class X board examinations and is finalized after the declaration of results.

Required Documents

  • i Copy of previous class mark sheet
  • ii School leaving certificate (TC)
  • iii Migration certificate, if applicable
  • iv Merit certificates for sports & co-curricular activities

Curriculum

The school proudly aligns with the CBSE curriculum with English as the primary medium of instruction, providing a holistic, enriching academic experience tailored for modern challenges.
