A Brief History of the School

Decades of educational excellence, values, and holistic growth.

Madam Kesari Devi Bajaj founded S.S.V Public School in 1950 with a vision to empower young minds through education, self-reliance, equality, and strong values.

S.S.V. Montessori School, founded with basic facilities, grew into a prestigious educational institution through the dedication of Madam Kesari Devi Bajaj and Chairman Mr. Naresh Kumar Bajaj. Their vision, sincerity, and commitment established S.S.V. School as a respected name across the city and NCR.

In recent years, S.S.V. School has continued its commitment to excellence, innovation, meaningful education, strong moral values, and social responsibility through its dedicated staff.

Vision

S.S.V School is committed to providing quality education, effective leadership and an environment that nurtures the holistic development of every child.

Mission

To impart multidimensional education and develop students into responsible citizens with confidence, creativity and leadership.

Values

Character building, discipline, honesty, integrity, patriotism, respect and compassion remain the foundation of our educational philosophy.

Culture

Students participate in cultural, literary, sports and co-curricular activities that strengthen confidence, teamwork and appreciation for Indian heritage.

Modern Infrastructure Architecture: The newly constructed four-storied campus equipped with elevators, digital classrooms, modern laboratories, smart library, sports infrastructure and student-friendly facilities provides an excellent learning environment.
