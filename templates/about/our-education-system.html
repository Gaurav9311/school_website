Our Education System

Experiential, skill-based, and technology-driven learning for future-ready global leaders.

A Word About Our Education System

Educational Set-Up: S.S.V School follows child-oriented methodology for all the classes, especially for pre-primary wing. The ECE concept along with integration of multiple intelligence brings out the best in the child. In primary classes special emphasis is on learning by doing' than on rote learning.

Education at all levels is viewed as acquisition of skills rather than bookish knowledge. The curriculum is based on imparting skills that can form the basis for a career later on. It is with this purpose that activities such as art and craft, photography, music, dance, drama, gymnastics, yoga, a wide range of fitness activities along with interesting tech savvy projects are an integral part of learning environment.

Nurturing the think tank of the nation from the very beginning, the school collaborates with new and scientific learning methodology like Jolly Phonics and Jodo Gyan for experiential learning.

ATAL Tinkering Lab & A.I / I.T

ATAL Tinkering Lab: Niti Aayog Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) has selected S.S.V School Public School for establishment of ATAL TINKERING LAB. It is a step towards a new India. It would contain educational "do it yourself" Kits and equipment on science, electronics, robotics, open source, microcontroller boards, sensors and 3D printers etc.

A.I / I.T: The future belongs to AI. In order to make the students self-reliant and future ready the school is running successfully AI training for classes VIII onwards.

Integration of Technology

NIIT Quick school is an integrated and comprehensive Educational Resource Planning solution that spans across all functional requirements and departments of the institution. The product aims to minimize the gap between parents and the school by empowering them with various techno savvy facilities and other real time data management which includes fees, examination, attendance, lesson plans etc.

The school is equipped with modern science laboratories, activity labs, a technology networked library, and a fully equipped audio recording venue for speaking and listening tests. Smart Boards across classrooms have brought about a revolutionary change in the teaching-learning environment.

Clubs and Creativity

S.S.V School Eco club strives at spreading awareness about nature and its conservation. Eco-friendly activities are frequently organized, including the adoption of neighborhood parks. 'Abhivyakti' is the special learning center created to foster novel learning environments in accordance with student abilities.

Diverse clubs such as Boosters (Sports/Yoga), Symphony (Dance/Music), Wizkid (Computer), Crazy Bite (Cooking), Tempest (Dramatics), Green Brigade (Eco Club), and Picassos (Art) offer hands-on experiences under professional guidance.

Astronomy Club & Legacy of Leadership

Astronomy Club: Created to inspire interest in the science of astronomy through observing the sun, moon, stars, and solar system wonders.

Legacy of Leadership: The "House System" (Sadbhawana, Sanskriti, Satkarm, and Sanskar) fosters healthy competition and teamwork. Annual investiture ceremonies appoint prefectorial teams, while Scout, Guide, and NCC Girls units instill strict discipline, self-reliance, and leadership.

Student Support System & Well-Being

Career guidance sessions, workshops by experts, and robust health programs ensure complete emotional, physical, and psychological child welfare.

Furthermore, extensive sports programs, professional football tournaments, STEM initiatives, and publications like *Kaleidoscope*, *Nirmal Jyoti*, and *Spectrum* provide students platforms to excel.
