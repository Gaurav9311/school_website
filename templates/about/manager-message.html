Shri Sant Vinoba School

From The
School Manager

S.S.V School Public School has completed three-fourths of a century of existence, consistently setting benchmarks of excellence, innovation, and quality education.

Our vision has always been to build an institution where values, academic excellence, sports, creativity, and character development go hand in hand. Every child is encouraged to discover their true potential through dedication, compassion, and continuous learning.

Shri Sant Vinoba School has deep roots in the educational landscape of Greater Noida. It is a matter of immense pride and privilege to be associated with this institution where students come with dreams and aspirations, and leave with confidence, knowledge, and strong values.

Mr. A.K. Kumar

Manager
