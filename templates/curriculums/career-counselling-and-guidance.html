Career Counselling

Helping students discover strengths, interests, and career opportunities through expert guidance.

Career Counselling plays a vital role in identifying the real potential of students and guiding them towards the right career path. Choosing an academic stream and career is one of the most important decisions in a student's life.

At S.S.V School, regular Career Counselling Workshops are organized for students of Classes X and XII. These sessions help students understand their interests, abilities, personality traits and career opportunities.

Renowned organizations such as Career Launcher, ABSB Guidance Classes, and other career experts conduct interactive sessions that provide valuable guidance regarding competitive examinations, higher education and future career planning.

These workshops enable students to confidently choose their own career path, reduce academic stress, and prepare themselves for a successful future.
