Visual & Performing Arts

Art inspires creativity, confidence, and self-expression through Visual and Performing Arts.

Creative Expression & Excellence

Students receive professional training in Dance, Theatre, Vocal Music and Instrumental Music. These activities help children develop confidence, creativity, communication skills and teamwork while enjoying the learning process.

Every year the school proudly celebrates World Dance Day as VIBRATION on 29th April. The event promotes unity, harmony, peace and humanity while encouraging students to express themselves through artistic performances.
