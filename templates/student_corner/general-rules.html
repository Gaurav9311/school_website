General Rules

Clear school rules ensure discipline, safety, respect, and a positive learning environment.

Student Rules

  • Use English for communication inside the school premises and school buses.
  • Private tuition is strictly discouraged as comprehensive academic support is provided.
  • Damage to school property must be fully compensated by the concerned student.
  • Students should not bring any gifts or tokens of appreciation for teachers or classmates.
  • Half-day leave requests will be granted exclusively during certified medical emergencies.
  • Objectionable literature, media, or inappropriate materials are strictly prohibited.
  • Students are solely responsible for the custody of their personal belongings.
  • Use of abusive, disrespectful, or inappropriate language is strictly prohibited.

Parent Guidelines

  • Ensure your child strictly wears the pristine prescribed school uniform daily.
  • Check, review, and countersign the school diary on a regular basis.
  • Compulsorily attend all scheduled Parent–Teacher Meetings (PTMs).
  • Meet faculty members exclusively during designated official visiting hours.
  • Always carry your authorized School Parent ID Card while visiting the campus.
  • Contact respected class teachers or coordinators directly for academic concerns.
  • Inform the school secretariat immediately about changes in communication address or phone number.
  • Discourage students from bringing mobile phones, expensive jewellery, or electronic gadgets.

Important Notice

  • The school entry gate closes sharp at 7:55 AM during the summer session and 9:35 AM during the winter session.
  • Late arrivals or students failing to comply with the proper uniform protocol will strictly not be permitted to attend school.
  • Electronic gadgets such as mobile phones, smartwatches, and expensive ornaments are completely prohibited inside the campus.
  • Unauthorized gadgets will be immediately confiscated and returned only to parents after the settlement of the applicable discipline fine.
  • Students are absolutely not permitted to ride motorized two-wheelers or vehicles to school under any circumstances.
