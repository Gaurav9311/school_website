School Discipline

Discipline builds character, responsibility, respect, and a positive learning environment.

General Discipline Rules

Students are expected to maintain the highest standards of discipline inside and outside the classroom. The following actions are considered serious violations of school discipline:

  • Any type of unruly behaviour, shouting, loitering in corridors, disobedience, habitual late coming, improper school uniform, and willful violation of school guidelines.
  • Students must take pristine care of school property. Damage caused intentionally or through sheer carelessness will result in strict disciplinary action and financial compensation.
  • Students should maintain proper decorum, respectful communication, and immaculate conduct. Aggressive behaviour, bullying, or use of offensive language will lead to immediate counselling followed by formal written disciplinary action.
🟡

Yellow Card Warning

Issued for repeated carelessness, untidy academic work, poor punctuality, improper school uniform protocols, and unsatisfactory performance monitored over a specific period of time.

🔴

Red Card Disciplinary Action

Issued when a student systematically fails to improve despite repeated official counselling and explicit warnings. Continued indiscipline will lead to suspension or expulsion as per school policy.
