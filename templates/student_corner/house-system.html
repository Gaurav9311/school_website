House System

The entire school community is divided into four premium houses— Satkarm, Sanskriti, Sadbhawana, and Sanskar. Each house promotes institutional leadership, core discipline, collaborative teamwork, confidence, and healthy competition through various thoughtfully curated inter-house activities conducted throughout the academic year.
